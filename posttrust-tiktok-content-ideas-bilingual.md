# PostTrust TikTok 选题库 / TikTok Content Ideas

> 本文档仅使用 PostTrust 当前代码中已经实现的功能策划内容，不宣传规划中或尚未上线的能力。
>
> Every idea in this document is based only on features currently implemented in PostTrust.

## 当前可宣传的功能 / Features Available Now

- LinkedIn Post 与 Article 两种审计模式 / Separate LinkedIn Post and Article audits
- 信任分、诊断结论与七类风险指标 / Trust score, verdict, and seven risk metrics
- 原文逐句高亮、风险解释和修改策略 / Sentence highlighting, risk explanations, and fix strategies
- Authentic 与 Conservative 两种改写 / Authentic and Conservative rewrites
- 可填写真实信息的方括号占位符 / Fillable placeholders for real facts
- 目标受众、发帖目标和保留语气选项 / Audience, post goal, and preserve-tone options
- 审计历史、重新打开与单条删除 / Audit history, reopen, and delete
- 基于 3–5 篇本人帖子生成一个 Voice Profile / One Voice Profile built from 3–5 original posts
- Voice Profile 可用于后续改写 / Voice Profile applied to future rewrites
- 免费首次审计、积分包和订阅 / First audit free, credit packs, and subscription

## 不应宣传的功能 / Features Not Available

- 判断内容是不是 AI 写的 / Detecting whether AI authored a post
- 自动发布或连接 LinkedIn 账号 / Auto-publishing or connecting a LinkedIn account
- 批量审计、浏览器插件或公开帖子抓取 / Batch audits, browser extension, or public-post scraping
- 自动生成虚构经历、数据或客户案例 / Inventing experiences, metrics, or customer stories
- 完整账号删除、数据导出或高级历史筛选 / Full account deletion, data export, or advanced history filters

## 选题 / Content Ideas

### 1. 这段话看起来很专业，但什么也没说 / This Sounds Professional but Says Nothing

- **Hook：**“如果删掉这句话，读者会损失任何信息吗？” / “If I delete this sentence, does the reader lose any information?”
- **演示：**粘贴一篇包含泛泛成功学表达的 Post，运行审计，展示 `Empty Language Density`、信任分和被高亮的原句。 / Paste a Post with generic success language, run an audit, and show the trust score, `Empty Language Density`, and highlighted sentence.
- **视频结构：**原句 5 秒 → 点击高亮查看为什么损害信任 → 切换到 Conservative Rewrite。 / Show the original for 5 seconds → click the highlight to explain the trust problem → switch to the Conservative Rewrite.
- **CTA：**“把你的草稿贴进 PostTrust，第一次审计免费。” / “Paste your draft into PostTrust. Your first audit is free.”

### 2. “Work Smarter, Not Harder” 为什么让你像 AI / Why “Work Smarter, Not Harder” Makes You Sound Like AI

- **Hook：**“这句话没有错，问题是任何人都能说。” / “This sentence is not wrong. The problem is that anyone could say it.”
- **演示：**用包含常见 cliché 的 Post 展示 `Clichés & Buzzwords` 指标、逐句风险解释和替换建议。 / Audit a Post containing a familiar cliché and show the `Clichés & Buzzwords` metric, sentence-level explanation, and replacement strategy.
- **视频结构：**圈出 cliché → 展示风险等级 → 对比原句与 Conservative Rewrite。 / Circle the cliché → reveal its risk level → compare the original with the Conservative Rewrite.
- **CTA：**“发布前先检查哪些句子听起来像模板。” / “Find the sentences that sound templated before you publish.”

### 3. 高信任分的 LinkedIn 帖子长什么样 / What a High-Trust LinkedIn Post Looks Like

- **Hook：**“专业感不是大词，而是可验证的细节。” / “Authority does not come from big words. It comes from verifiable details.”
- **演示：**审计一篇包含具体时间、数字和行动的 Post，展示较高信任分、诊断结论与低风险指标。 / Audit a Post containing dates, numbers, and concrete actions; show its higher trust score, verdict, and low-risk metrics.
- **视频结构：**先猜分数 → 揭晓信任分 → 快速扫过七类指标。 / Ask viewers to guess the score → reveal it → scan the seven metrics.
- **CTA：**“你觉得自己的下一篇能拿多少分？” / “What score would your next post get?”

### 4. 低分不是因为 AI，而是因为缺少证据 / A Low Score Does Not Mean AI. It Means Weak Evidence

- **Hook：**“PostTrust 不判断谁写的，它判断读者为什么不信。” / “PostTrust does not guess who wrote it. It shows why readers may not trust it.”
- **演示：**展示首页说明、Missing Evidence/Data 指标和针对具体句子的风险解释。 / Show the product explanation, Missing Evidence/Data metric, and a sentence-level trust explanation.
- **视频结构：**澄清不是 AI detector → 展示缺证据句子 → 展示修复策略。 / Clarify that it is not an AI detector → show an unsupported sentence → reveal the fix strategy.
- **CTA：**“审计你的可信度，不是猜测作者身份。” / “Audit credibility instead of guessing authorship.”

### 5. 一句话暴露伪专家感 / The Sentence That Makes You Sound Like a Fake Expert
- **Hook：**“命令读者之前，先证明你做过。” / “Before lecturing readers, prove you have done the work.”
- **画面与功能：**审计说教式 Post，点击 Fake-Expert Signals 高亮、解释和修改策略。 / Audit a preachy Post; open its Fake-Expert Signals highlight, explanation, and fix.
- **CTA：**“发布前找出伪专家句子。” / “Find fake-expert language before publishing.”

### 6. 把观点变成经历，只差三个问题 / Three Questions That Turn an Opinion into Experience
- **Hook：**“观点没错，读者只是不知道你凭什么说。” / “Your point may be right. Readers just do not know what you experienced.”
- **画面与功能：**展示 Missing Lived Experience 和 Clarifying details needed 问题。 / Show Missing Lived Experience and the Clarifying details needed questions.
- **CTA：**“工具负责追问，事实必须来自你。” / “Let the tool ask; the facts must come from you.”

### 7. 一行一句为什么越来越像模板 / Why One-Line Paragraphs Feel Templated
